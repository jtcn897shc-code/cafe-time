# Phase 3 research: Cafe Time Abbotsford + Abbotsford cafe reviews

## Method
Web search across Google-indexed review aggregators, Yelp listings, Tripadvisor, Uber Eats, Instagram, and local Abbotsford "best cafe" round-ups. Cafe Time itself has a thin public review footprint (small independent shop), so findings blend direct mentions of Cafe Time with the recurring language Abbotsford cafe-goers use generally. Treated as buyer-voice input for copy, not as verbatim quotes to publish.

## Direct Cafe Time mentions
- "Breakfast sandwiches and Korean rice bowls are seriously amazing"
- "Specialty coffees are the best in Abbotsford"
- Tiramisu latte called "incredible" / "god-tier"
- "Smooth, rich, full-bodied flavor" (coffee)
- "Friendly staff", "feels like home"
- "Community hub where coffee lovers unite"
- "Aesthetic touches that create a cozy yet vibrant atmosphere"
- "Regulars appreciate the owner's commitment to quality and community engagement"
- Good mix of vegetarian and non-vegetarian sandwiches
- Location note: near Abbotsford City Hall, South Fraser Way

## General Abbotsford independent-cafe review language (recurring phrases)
- "Cozy" appears constantly, paired with "warm the moment you step in"
- "Abundant natural light" / "large windows that invite natural light"
- "Quiet music" and "calming piano music" for focus/relax visits
- "Comfortable tables", "available wifi" (work-from-cafe visitors)
- "Perfect for relaxing with friends or enjoying quiet time with a good book"
- "Chic, spacious, modern decor... welcoming for relaxation or productivity"
- "Passionate about coffee and community"
- Recurring objection/hesitation pattern across chains vs. independents: people explicitly seek out the "not-a-chain" feel, praise home-roasted / organic sourcing, and call out fresh-daily food as a differentiator from grab-and-go chains

## What this buys the copy (buyer's-own-words takeaways)
1. Pain/objection: "another basic coffee run" fatigue vs. chains -> lean into real sourcing (Agro Roasters, Tego, Cobs Bread), not a logo.
2. Desired outcome: a "slow down" moment, natural light, cozy, feels like home, good for lingering with people they love.
3. Trust language: fresh daily, local, organic, rewards for loyalty.
4. Sensory hook: iced/matcha and specialty coffee praised as best-in-area; cold drinks are a draw.
5. Register: short, warm, confident, not corporate. Matches the plan's "bold and modern" direction: punchy claims, plain nouns, little chip labels rather than paragraphs.
